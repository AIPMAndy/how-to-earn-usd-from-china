# Earn USD From China — A Practical Guide

[**Read the live website**](https://aipmandy.github.io/how-to-earn-usd-from-china/) · [中文](README.md) · [Full Chinese guide](GUIDE.md) · [Official sources](docs/SOURCES.md)

A Chinese-language guide for professionals in mainland China looking to offer genuine services and products to overseas customers. Written by **AI Chief Andy**, drawing on experience in B2B AI products, career consulting, content and open source.

The guide covers five real questions: **what to sell, where to find clients, how to quote and deliver, how to get paid legally, and what business records and taxes may apply.** No guaranteed-income claims.

## Eight practical paths

| Route | Typical sellers | Deliverable |

| AI automation and development | Developers and AI practitioners | Working workflow or internal tool |
| Design and video | Designers and editors | Landing page, brand assets, videos |
| Content and growth | Marketers and SEO specialists | Content and analysis |
| China sourcing support | Procurement and manufacturing specialists | Supplier research and coordination |
| Localization | Bilingual domain experts | Product and technical content |
| Digital products | People with reusable expertise | Templates, teaching resources, tools |
| Micro-SaaS | Teams prepared to maintain software | Subscription product |
