# 英文客户沟通模板

这些是可替换的商业沟通示例，适合专业咨询、开发、设计和运营服务。使用时需要依据自己真实经历修改，不能虚构案例、雇主或结果。

## 1. 个人介绍

Hi, I'm Andy. I've worked on AI products and B2B services for years. I help teams turn specific business problems into practical workflows and deliverables. If you're working on something similar, I'd be happy to learn more about your needs.

## 2. 初次私信

Hi [Name], I saw your post about [specific problem]. I work on [your relevant skill] and have an idea that may help. Would you be open to a short chat? I can share a few concrete options based on what you've already tried.

## 3. 项目提案

Hi [Name], thanks for sharing the details. Based on your brief, I suggest we start with [specific scope]. The deliverables would include [A], [B] and [C]. I can send a timeline and fixed quote once we confirm the requirements and access permissions. Let me know what outcome matters most to you.

## 4. 交付和验收

Hi [Name], I've completed the agreed scope and attached [deliverables]. I also included setup notes and known limitations. Please review the acceptance items in our agreement and let me know if anything needs clarification.

## 5. 后续合作

Hi [Name], glad the first project is complete. If this workflow continues to be useful, we can discuss a maintenance arrangement or a second phase based on your priorities.

记住只有真实约定和真实交付才能写进商务沟通，付款平台必须符合双方主体的实际资格。
